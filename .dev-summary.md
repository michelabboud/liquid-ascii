## 🎤 TTS Summary

**You asked: "Do I need a tiny LLM for text-to-speech?"**

**Answer: No!**

- **LLMs** generate text (like GPT, Claude)
- **TTS** converts text → audio (what you need)
- **You already have TTS** with 47 English voices!

**Current System:**
- ✅ edge-tts (Microsoft) - online, free, best quality
- ✅ 322 total voices (100+ languages)
- ✅ Perfect lip sync with word timing
- ✅ Works immediately, no setup needed

**Optional Offline Alternatives:**
- pyttsx3 (system voices, ~50KB) - see src/audio/tts_offline.py
- Piper TTS (neural quality, ~50-100MB) - see docs/TTS_OPTIONS.md
- Coqui TTS (best quality, ~500MB) - see docs/TTS_OPTIONS.md

**Recommendation:** Stick with edge-tts (current) - it's the best option!
